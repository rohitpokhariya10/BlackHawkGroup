FAQ 1 | How is property value estimated? 
A property estimate may consider recent comparable sales, location, square footage, condition, 
upgrades, and current buyer demand. Different valuation methods can produce different results. 
FAQ 2 | Why might estimates differ? 
Automated tools may not fully capture renovations, interior condition, unique features, or recent 
local transactions. A more detailed review can provide additional context. 
FAQ 3 | Can my property value change? 
Yes. Market conditions, financing costs, inventory, property condition, and neighborhood activity 
can influence value over time. 


--- PAGE 6 ---
FAQ 4 | Is the estimated price guaranteed? 
No. An estimate is not a guaranteed sale price. Final outcomes depend on buyer interest, 
market conditions, negotiation, and the terms of a transaction. 
FAQ 5 | Does asking about value mean I must sell? 
No. You can request information to help evaluate your options without committing to a listing 
agreement.
