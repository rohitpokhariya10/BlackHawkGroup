MEET YOUR BROKER
Quiana Shonté Echols
With millions of dollars in real estate sales and a reputation built on excellence, Quiana Shonte Echols is the Founder, Broker, and visionary leader behind Watch Realty Co.

Her expertise spans luxury real estate, new construction, investment properties, and brokerage leadership, making her a trusted advisor to clients, developers, and real estate professionals alike.

Known for her strategic mindset, market knowledge, and commitment to delivering exceptional results, Quiana has built Watch Realty Co. into a brokerage focused on professionalism, innovation, and agent success. She is passionate about creating opportunities for both clients and agents while maintaining the highest standards of service and integrity.

As a broker, mentor, and entrepreneur, Quiana is dedicated to cultivating a culture of excellence, collaboration, and growth. She believes that great real estate professionals are built through leadership, accountability, and continuous education.

Quiana looks forward to welcoming exceptional new partners to Watch Realty Co. and continuing to build a brokerage where agents can thrive, grow their businesses, and create lasting success.
